# Lesson 02 — Your First Chat with Claude

⏱️ **10 minutes** · Level: Beginner · Needs: a web browser (Edge or Chrome)

## 🧠 Theory (2 min)

The fastest way to use Claude is in your **web browser** at **claude.ai**. Nothing to install. You type a message, Claude replies, and the whole back-and-forth is a **conversation**. Claude remembers everything *within that one conversation* (up to its context window), but a **new chat starts fresh**.

**Free vs. paid:** There's a free tier that's plenty for learning. Paid plans (Pro) give you more usage and access to the most capable models. You can do this entire course on free/Pro — you do **not** need the paid API until Lesson 9.

---

## 🛠️ Practice (6 min)

### Step 1 — Create your account

1. Open **Microsoft Edge** (or Chrome).
2. Go to **https://claude.ai**
3. Click **Sign up**. You can use your Google account or an email address.
4. Verify your email if asked, and complete the short sign-up.

### Step 2 — Send your first message

You'll see a text box at the bottom that says something like *"How can I help you today?"*.

Copy this and paste it in, then press **Enter**:

```text
Hi Claude! I'm brand new to AI. In 5 short bullet points, explain what you can help me with in everyday life. Keep it simple.
```

Read the reply. Congratulations — you just used AI. 🎉

### Step 3 — Have a real back-and-forth

Now ask a follow-up **in the same chat** so you see that Claude remembers context:

```text
Great. Now pick bullet #1 and give me one concrete example I could try today.
```

Notice it built on its previous answer without you re-explaining. That's the "conversation memory" within a chat.

### Step 4 — Start a new chat

Click **New chat** (top-left, usually a ✏️ or "+" icon). Ask:

```text
What did we just talk about?
```

It won't know — because this is a fresh conversation. **Lesson learned:** keep related work in one chat; start a new chat for a new topic.

## 🗺️ Quick tour of the screen

| Area | What it does |
|------|--------------|
| **Left sidebar** | Your past conversations + "New chat" |
| **Message box** | Where you type. **Shift+Enter** = new line, **Enter** = send |
| **Model picker** | Usually near the top; lets you choose Haiku/Sonnet/Opus |
| **📎 Attach** | Upload files and images (Lesson 4) |

## ✅ Checkpoint

- [ ] You have a claude.ai account.
- [ ] You sent a message and got a reply.
- [ ] You understand that a **new chat forgets** the old one.

---

## 🎯 Homework

Take the "most annoying text task" from Lesson 01's homework and just **describe it to Claude**, ending with: *"Ask me any questions you need before helping."* Notice how it asks smart clarifying questions. That habit — letting Claude ask first — makes answers far better.

---

## 💡 Key takeaways

- claude.ai = zero-install way to use Claude.
- One conversation = shared memory; new chat = clean slate.
- **Shift+Enter** for multi-line prompts.
