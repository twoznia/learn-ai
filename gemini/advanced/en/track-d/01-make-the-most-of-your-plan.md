## 🧠 Theory (5 min)

There are **two different ways to pay for Gemini**, and mixing them up is the most common beginner mistake:

| | **Subscription (Google AI Pro / Ultra)** | **API (pay-per-use)** |
|---|---|---|
| What it is | A plan for the **Gemini app**, **Gemini in Workspace**, and the **Gemini CLI** | A **developer** service, metered by usage |
| You pay | A **fixed monthly price** (Free tier exists) | Per request beyond the free tier |
| Best for | Chatting, Gems, Deep Research, Canvas, everyday CLI use | Building your own apps that call Gemini |
| Extra cost per use? | **No** — included in your plan | **Yes** beyond the free limits |

This whole track uses the **subscription** — no API key required. Everything you do is covered by your plan.

### The plans

- **Free** — try Gemini, daily limits, the fast everyday model.
- **Google AI Pro** — higher limits, access to the most capable model, **Deep Research**, **Canvas**, **Gems**, and Gemini across Google apps.
- **Google AI Ultra** — the highest limits and earliest access to the newest features (video generation, the biggest models). Best if you hit Pro's limits or want everything.

Plus: **Gemini in Google Workspace** — Gemini inside Gmail, Docs, Sheets, Slides, and Meet — is part of the app experience, not the API.

> 💡 The **API** (a key from Google AI Studio) is a *separate*, developer path with its own metering. If a tutorial says "get an API key and set up billing," that's not needed for anything in this track.

## 🛠️ Practice (9 min)

### Step 1 — Find your plan

Open the **Gemini app** ([gemini.google.com](https://gemini.google.com)) → your profile (top-right) → **Settings / Upgrade**. Note whether you're on Free, Pro, or Ultra.

### Step 2 — Tour what your plan unlocks

In the Gemini app, find these (availability depends on plan):

- The **model picker** (top of the chat) — a **fast** model and a **most-capable** model.
- **Gems** — your custom assistants (Track A1).
- **Deep Research** and **Canvas** (Track A3).
- The **tools / connected apps** menu — extensions to Google apps.

Just locate them for now — the rest of this track uses each.

### Step 3 — Pick the right model for the job

Open the **model picker** and read the descriptions. The rule of thumb:

| Model | Use it for |
|-------|-----------|
| **Flash (fast)** | Everyday questions, quick tasks — fast and efficient |
| **Pro (most capable)** | Hard reasoning, big documents, careful analysis |

> Exact model names in the picker change over time. The pattern holds: a **fast** everyday model and a **most-capable** model for hard work.

Choosing the fast model when you don't need the heaviest one makes your usage last longer (more in D3).

### Step 4 — Notice Gemini in Workspace

If you use Gmail/Docs, look for the Gemini side panel there. Same brain, right where you work — "summarize this thread," "draft a reply," "help me write this doc." All part of the plan, no API.

### Step 5 — Decide: do you actually need the API?

- **Chatting/working with Gemini for yourself?** → Subscription. Done.
- **Building an app that calls Gemini for other people?** → API (Tracks B and C cover it — a separate, metered path).

For this track: **stay on the subscription.**

## 🧩 Subscription vs API — quick decision

| You want to… | Use |
|--------------|-----|
| Chat, research, write, analyze files | Subscription |
| Use Gems, Deep Research, Canvas | Subscription |
| Gemini inside Gmail/Docs/Sheets | Subscription (Workspace) |
| Use the Gemini CLI on your machine | Subscription (free tier / plan) |
| Ship an app that calls Gemini for users | API |

---

## 💡 Key takeaways

- The **subscription** (Pro/Ultra) is a flat price with **no per-use cost**; the **API** is a separate, metered developer path.
- Your plan already includes Gems, Deep Research, Canvas, Gemini in Workspace, and the CLI — this track uses them without an API key.
- Match the **model** to the task; the fast model when you can afford it stretches your usage.
