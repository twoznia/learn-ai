## 🧠 Theory (4 min)

The last "beyond chat" surfaces are about **talking and listening** instead of typing:

- **Gemini Live** — a real-time **voice conversation** with Gemini. You speak, it speaks back, you interrupt and steer naturally — hands-free. On mobile you can also **share your camera or screen** so it sees what you see.
- **Audio Overviews** — Gemini turns a topic or your documents into a short, **listenable audio summary** (a two-host discussion), so you can absorb material while walking or commuting.

These change *when* and *how* you use Gemini — on the go, eyes-free, or while multitasking.

> Availability of Live and Audio Overviews varies by plan, device, and region, and rolls out over time. If you don't see one, the others in this track still apply.

## 🛠️ Practice (10 min)

### Step 1 — Start a Live conversation

In the Gemini **mobile** app, start **Gemini Live** (a voice/Live button). Then just talk:

```text
(spoken) Help me plan a simple weeknight dinner using chicken, rice, and whatever's common.
Ask me one question at a time.
```

Notice you can **interrupt** and redirect mid-sentence — it's a conversation, not a form.

### Step 2 — Use it hands-free for real tasks

Great Live use cases:
- **Brainstorming** out loud on a walk.
- **Practicing** a talk or an interview ("ask me tough questions").
- **Thinking through** a decision while your hands are busy.

```text
(spoken) I'm rehearsing a 2-minute intro about myself for a job interview.
Let me practice, then give me one concrete improvement.
```

### Step 3 — Share your camera (if supported)

On supported devices, share the **camera** so Gemini sees what you point at:

```text
(spoken, pointing camera) What is this plant, and how often should I water it?
```

Eyes-free help grounded in the real world around you.

### Step 4 — Generate an Audio Overview

If available (in the app, or via **Deep Research** reports / NotebookLM), turn material into audio:

```text
Give me an audio overview of this report so I can listen to the key points.
```

You get a short spoken summary you can play while doing something else.

### Step 5 — Match the surface to the moment

| Moment | Use |
|--------|-----|
| Hands busy / walking | **Gemini Live** (voice) |
| Need to *see* something | Live + camera share |
| Commuting / away from screen | **Audio Overview** (listen) |
| Careful editing / precise work | Regular typed chat |

### Step 6 — Privacy awareness

> ⚠️ Voice, camera, and screen sharing send what you show/say to Gemini. Don't share sensitive documents, faces, or surroundings you wouldn't want processed. Turn sharing off when you're done.

## 💡 Key takeaways

- **Gemini Live** is a real-time **voice** conversation (with optional camera/screen) — great hands-free and for practice.
- **Audio Overviews** turn topics/documents into a **listenable** summary for on-the-go.
- Match the **surface to the moment**, and mind **privacy** when sharing voice, camera, or screen.
