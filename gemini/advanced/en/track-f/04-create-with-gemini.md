# F4 — Create with Gemini: Images & Video

## 🧠 Theory (4 min)

Gemini isn't just text — it can **generate media**:

- **Images** — describe a picture and Gemini creates it (Google's **Imagen** models). Great for illustrations, mockups, social posts, concept art.
- **Video** — describe a short clip and Gemini generates it (Google's **Veo** models). Often on higher tiers (Ultra) or rolling out.

The skill is the same as prompting for text: **be specific** — subject, style, mood, composition, colors — and **iterate**. Vague prompts give generic results; precise prompts give what you pictured.

> Image and especially video generation depend on your plan and region, and availability changes over time. If a feature isn't there, the **prompting skills** still transfer.

---

## 🛠️ Practice (10 min)

### Step 1 — Generate an image

In the Gemini app, ask for a picture with real detail:

```text
Create an image: a cozy reading nook by a rainy window, warm lamplight,
a cat asleep on a stack of books, soft watercolor style, muted autumn colors.
```

Notice how much the **specifics** (style, light, mood) shape the result.

### Step 2 — Iterate like a director

Don't accept the first take — refine:

```text
Same scene, but make it evening, add a steaming mug on the windowsill,
and a warmer color palette.
```

You're directing — subject, lighting, palette, framing are all yours to adjust.

### Step 3 — Use images for real tasks

```text
Make 3 simple icon-style images for a to-do app: "add task", "done", "reminder".
Flat, single color, clean lines, transparent-looking background.
```

Concept art, mockups, social graphics, slide visuals — all describable.

### Step 4 — Try video (if available)

If your plan includes video generation:

```text
Generate a short clip: a paper airplane gliding slowly across a sunny office,
soft focus, gentle camera follow, 5 seconds.
```

Same rule — describe subject, motion, camera, mood, length.

### Step 5 — Prompt like a pro

A strong media prompt names:
- **Subject** — what's in it.
- **Style** — watercolor, photo, flat icon, 3D…
- **Mood/light** — warm, moody, bright.
- **Composition** — close-up, wide, centered.
- **Details** — colors, era, extras.

### Step 6 — Use responsibly

> ⚠️ Generated media can mislead if presented as real. Don't create images/video that impersonate real people, fabricate events, or could deceive. Label AI-generated content when you share it, and respect others' likeness and copyright.

## 🧩 Media prompt recipe

| Part | Example |
|------|---------|
| Subject | "a paper airplane" |
| Style | "soft watercolor" / "photorealistic" |
| Mood / light | "warm evening light" |
| Composition | "wide shot, centered" |
| Details | "muted autumn colors, 5 seconds" |

## ✅ Checkpoint

- [ ] You generated an image with a specific, detailed prompt.
- [ ] You iterated on it (changed light/mood/palette).
- [ ] You made images for a real use (icons, mockup, graphic).
- [ ] You tried video if available, and you know the responsible-use rules.

---

## 🎯 Homework

Create a small set of images for something real you're doing (a slide, a post, an app mockup). Iterate each at least once. Write down the prompt recipe that worked so you can reuse it.

---

## 💡 Key takeaways

- Gemini generates **images (Imagen)** and **video (Veo)** — availability varies by plan; the prompting skills always transfer.
- **Be specific** (subject, style, mood, composition, details) and **iterate like a director**.
- Use generated media **responsibly** — no impersonation or deception; label and respect likeness/copyright.
