# Lesson 05 — Gemini in Chrome and Google

## 🧠 Theory (2 min)

Unlike some AIs, Gemini doesn't need a separate desktop app on Windows — it's **built into products you already use**. Learning where it lives means you can reach for AI without opening a new tab:

- **Chrome** — Gemini in the browser (side panel / address bar on recent versions).
- **Google Search** — AI overviews and AI Mode summarize results.
- **Google Workspace** — Gemini inside Gmail, Docs, Sheets, and Slides (availability depends on your account/plan).

This lesson is a quick tour so you know your options. Don't worry if one surface isn't available on your account — the web app from Lesson 2 always works.

---

## 🛠️ Practice (7 min)

### Option A — Gemini in Chrome

1. Make sure **Chrome is up to date**: menu **⋮ → Help → About Google Chrome**, let it update, relaunch.
2. Sign in to Chrome with your Google account (top-right avatar).
3. Look for the **Gemini spark icon** (often top-right or in the side panel). Click it.
4. Ask about the page you're on:

```text
Summarize this page in 3 bullet points and tell me if it's worth reading fully.
```

> If you don't see the icon, that's fine — features roll out gradually. Use gemini.google.com in a tab instead.

### Option B — Google Search (AI overviews)

1. Go to **google.com** and search something explanatory, e.g. *"how does compound interest work"*.
2. Read the **AI overview** at the top — it's Gemini summarizing sources.
3. Look for links/sources under the overview and click one to verify. **Always sanity-check AI summaries against a source.**

### Option C — Google Workspace (if available)

1. Open **Gmail** or **Google Docs**.
2. Look for a **Gemini / "Help me write"** button (a spark icon in the sidebar or toolbar).
3. In Docs, try:

```text
Help me write a short, friendly announcement about our team lunch next Friday at noon.
```

4. In Gmail's compose window, try **"Help me write"** to draft a reply.

> Workspace Gemini features depend on your Google plan (personal vs Workspace). If it's not there, no problem — you'll do the same tasks in Lesson 6 via the web app.

## 🧭 Where Gemini lives — quick map

| Surface | Good for | Always available? |
|---------|----------|-------------------|
| gemini.google.com | Anything, full chat | ✅ Yes |
| Chrome side panel | Asking about the current page | Rolling out |
| Google Search overviews | Quick explanations while searching | ✅ Mostly |
| Gmail / Docs / Sheets | Drafting & summarizing in place | Depends on plan |

## 🎯 Homework

Next time you search Google for an explanation, read the AI overview **and** click one source to confirm it. Building the "verify" habit early makes you a smart AI user.

---

## 💡 Key takeaways

- Gemini is woven into Chrome, Search, and Workspace — not just a website.
- The web app (Lesson 2) always works if a surface isn't on your account.
- Always verify AI overviews against a real source.
