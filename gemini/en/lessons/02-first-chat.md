# Lesson 02 — Your First Chat with Gemini

⏱️ **10 minutes** · Level: Beginner · Needs: a web browser + a Google account

## 🧠 Theory (2 min)

The fastest way to use Gemini is in your **web browser** at **gemini.google.com**. Nothing to install — you sign in with your **Google account**. You type a message, Gemini replies, and the whole back-and-forth is a **conversation**. Gemini remembers everything *within that one conversation*, but a **new chat starts fresh**.

**Free vs. paid:** There's a free tier that's plenty for learning. Paid plans (Google AI Pro/Ultra, often bundled with Google One) give more usage and access to the most capable models. You can do this whole course on the free tier.

---

## 🛠️ Practice (6 min)

### Step 1 — Open Gemini

1. Open **Chrome** (or Edge).
2. Go to **https://gemini.google.com**
3. **Sign in** with your Google account (the same one you use for Gmail). If you don't have one, create a free Google account first.

### Step 2 — Send your first message

You'll see a text box at the bottom. Copy this in and press **Enter**:

```text
Hi Gemini! I'm brand new to AI. In 5 short bullet points, explain what you can help me with in everyday life. Keep it simple.
```

Read the reply. Congratulations — you just used AI. 🎉

### Step 3 — Have a real back-and-forth

In the **same chat**, send a follow-up:

```text
Great. Now pick bullet #1 and give me one concrete example I could try today.
```

Notice it built on its previous answer — that's conversation memory.

### Step 4 — Start a new chat

Click **New chat** (top-left). Ask:

```text
What did we just talk about?
```

It won't know — this is a fresh conversation. **Lesson:** keep related work in one chat; start a new chat for a new topic.

---

## 🗺️ Quick tour of the screen

| Area | What it does |
|------|--------------|
| **Left sidebar** | Recent chats, "New chat", and Gems (Lesson 7) |
| **Message box** | Where you type. **Shift+Enter** = new line, **Enter** = send |
| **Model picker** | Near the top; switch between Fast (Flash) and more capable (Pro) |
| **➕ / image icon** | Add files and images (Lesson 4) |
| **🎤 mic** | Talk to Gemini instead of typing |

## ✅ Checkpoint

- [ ] You're signed in at gemini.google.com.
- [ ] You sent a message and got a reply.
- [ ] You understand a **new chat forgets** the old one.

## 🎯 Homework

Take your "most annoying text task" from Lesson 01 and just **describe it to Gemini**, ending with: *"Ask me any questions you need before helping."* Letting Gemini ask first makes answers far better.

---

## 💡 Key takeaways

- gemini.google.com = zero-install way to use Gemini; sign in with Google.
- One conversation = shared memory; new chat = clean slate.
- **Shift+Enter** for multi-line prompts; you can also use the mic.
