## 🧠 Theory (4 min)

**Advanced Voice Mode** lets you have a **real-time spoken conversation** with ChatGPT. You talk, it talks back with natural, expressive speech, and you can **interrupt and steer** mid-sentence — hands-free. On mobile you can also **share your camera or screen** so it sees what you're looking at.

This changes *when* and *how* you use GPT — on the go, eyes-free, or while your hands are busy:

- **Brainstorm** out loud on a walk.
- **Practice** a talk, an interview, or a language.
- **Get help** with something in front of you via the camera.

> Advanced Voice availability, camera/screen sharing, and limits vary by plan, device, and region and roll out over time. If a feature isn't there, the rest of this track still applies.

---

## 🛠️ Practice (10 min)

### Step 1 — Start a voice conversation

In the ChatGPT app, tap the **voice** button and just talk:

```text
(spoken) Help me plan a simple weeknight dinner using chicken, rice, and
whatever's common. Ask me one question at a time.
```

Notice you can **interrupt** and redirect mid-sentence — it's a conversation, not a form.

### Step 2 — Use it hands-free for real tasks

```text
(spoken) I'm rehearsing a 2-minute intro about myself for a job interview.
Let me practice, then give me one concrete improvement.
```

Great for rehearsing, thinking out loud, or learning while your hands are busy.

### Step 3 — Practice a language

Voice is excellent for speaking practice:

```text
(spoken) Let's practice ordering coffee in Spanish. You be the barista.
Correct my mistakes gently after each exchange.
```

### Step 4 — Share your camera (if supported)

On supported devices, share the **camera** so ChatGPT sees what you point at:

```text
(spoken, pointing camera) What is this plant, and how often should I water it?
```

Eyes-free help grounded in the real world around you.

### Step 5 — Match the surface to the moment

| Moment | Use |
|--------|-----|
| Hands busy / walking | **Advanced Voice** |
| Need to *see* something | Voice + camera share |
| Speaking/language practice | **Advanced Voice** |
| Careful editing / precise work | Regular typed chat |

### Step 6 — Privacy awareness

> ⚠️ Voice, camera, and screen sharing send what you show/say to ChatGPT. Don't share sensitive documents, faces, or surroundings you wouldn't want processed. Stop sharing when you're done.

## 💡 Key takeaways

- **Advanced Voice Mode** is a real-time **spoken** conversation (with optional camera/screen) — great hands-free and for practice.
- Match the **surface to the moment** — voice for on-the-go and rehearsal, typing for precise work.
- Mind **privacy** when sharing voice, camera, or screen — stop sharing when done.
