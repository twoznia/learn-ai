## 🧠 Theory (4 min)

**Sora** is OpenAI's **video generation** model: describe a short clip and it creates it. Useful for social content, concept videos, animations, and quick visual ideas.

Prompting video builds on image prompting (F3), plus two new dimensions:

- **Motion** — what moves, and how (slow drift, fast pan, a character walking).
- **Camera** — the shot and its movement (wide, close-up, follow, static).

Plus the usual: subject, style, mood, lighting, and **length** (clips are short).

> Sora availability, clip length, and monthly limits vary by plan and region and change over time. If you don't have access, the **prompting skills** here still apply, and the image lesson (F3) works on any plan.

---

## 🛠️ Practice (10 min)

### Step 1 — Generate a first clip

In Sora (or the video option in ChatGPT), describe a simple, concrete scene:

```text
A paper airplane glides slowly across a sunny office, soft focus,
gentle camera follow, calm mood, about 5 seconds.
```

Keep the first prompt simple — one subject, one clear motion.

### Step 2 — Add motion and camera language

Refine by naming *how things move* and *how the camera moves*:

```text
Same scene, but the airplane loops once near the window, and the camera
slowly pushes in. Warmer light.
```

### Step 3 — Match style to purpose

```text
A 5-second looping animation of a coffee cup with rising steam, flat 2D
illustration style, pastel colors, centered — for a cafe social post.
```

### Step 4 — Iterate in small steps

Video is sensitive to over-stuffed prompts. Change **one thing at a time** — motion, then camera, then style — and regenerate. You're directing, take by take.

### Step 5 — A prompt recipe for video

| Part | Example |
|------|---------|
| Subject | "a paper airplane" |
| Motion | "glides slowly, loops once" |
| Camera | "gentle follow, slow push-in" |
| Style | "soft focus, warm light" |
| Length | "~5 seconds" |

### Step 6 — Use responsibly

> ⚠️ Generated video is powerful and easy to misuse. Don't create clips that impersonate real people, fabricate events, or could deceive. Respect likeness and copyright, follow the tool's content rules, and **label AI-generated video** when you share it.

## 🧩 Image vs video prompting

| Adds… | Image (F3) | Video (Sora) |
|-------|-----------|--------------|
| Subject, style, mood | ✅ | ✅ |
| Composition | ✅ | ✅ |
| **Motion** | — | ✅ |
| **Camera movement** | — | ✅ |
| **Length** | — | ✅ (short) |

---

## ✅ Checkpoint

- [ ] You generated a short clip from a simple prompt (or practiced the recipe if you lack access).
- [ ] You refined it with **motion** and **camera** language.
- [ ] You matched a style to a real purpose.
- [ ] You can state the responsible-use rules for generated video.

---

## 🎯 Homework

If you have Sora access, make one short clip for something real (a post, an intro, a concept). Iterate it three times, changing one thing each time. Save the prompt recipe that worked. No access? Write three detailed video prompts using the recipe so you're ready when you do.

---

## 💡 Key takeaways

- **Sora** generates short **video** from a description — availability varies by plan.
- Video prompting adds **motion**, **camera**, and **length** on top of image-prompting skills; iterate one change at a time.
- Use generated video **responsibly** — no impersonation or deception; respect likeness/copyright and label it.
