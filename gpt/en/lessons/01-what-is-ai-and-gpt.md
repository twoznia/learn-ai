## 🧠 Theory (4 min)

### What "AI" means here

When people say **AI** today, they usually mean a **Large Language Model (LLM)** — a program trained on huge amounts of text so it can **predict the next words** in a sentence. Done at massive scale, that produces something that can write, summarize, explain, translate, and code.

Think of it as an extremely well-read assistant that:

- has learned from a huge portion of the public internet and many books,
- is great at language, reasoning, and drafting,
- but **doesn't truly "know" facts** — it predicts likely text, so it can be confidently wrong (a **hallucination**).

> 🔑 **Golden rule:** AI is a brilliant assistant, not an oracle. Use it to draft, explain, and speed up — then verify anything important.

### What ChatGPT and GPT are

**GPT** (Generative Pre-trained Transformer) is the family of AI models made by **OpenAI**. **ChatGPT** is the app you chat with — the friendly front door to those GPT models. You can use GPT in several ways, all covered in this course:

| Way to use GPT | What it is | Lesson |
|-------------------|-----------|--------|
| **chatgpt.com** | Chat in your browser | 02 |
| **ChatGPT desktop app** | A Windows app | 05 |
| **API** | GPT inside your own code | 09–11 |
| **Codex** | AI that edits code in your terminal | 13 |

### The GPT model family

GPT comes in sizes. Pick based on the job:

| Model type | Best for | Feel |
|------------|----------|------|
| **nano / mini** | Fast, cheap, simple tasks | Quick and light |
| **standard (e.g. GPT-5)** | Everyday work & hard reasoning | The capable default |
| **reasoning modes** | Step-by-step problem solving | Thinks longer, more accurate |

You don't need to memorize version numbers. Remember: **mini = fast and cheap**, **the flagship (GPT-5) = smartest**. In ChatGPT, OpenAI usually picks a sensible default for you, and you can switch models from a menu.

### Tokens (the unit AI counts in)

AI reads and writes in **tokens** — chunks of text (~¾ of a word). Why care?

1. **Limits** — a model can only "see" so many tokens at once (its **context window**).
2. **Cost** — with the API you pay per token (tiny fractions of a cent per request while learning).

## 🛠️ Practice (4 min)

You don't need an account yet. Build intuition:

1. Open a notepad or paper.
2. Write **three tasks** in your life or work that involve *words*: writing, explaining, summarizing, planning, or looking things up.
3. Next to each, note whether you'd trust the AI's answer **as-is** or **verify it** (numbers, laws, medical, money → verify).

Keep this list. By Lesson 6 you'll be doing all three with ChatGPT.

## 💡 Key takeaways

- AI = a language model that predicts text; brilliant but not infallible.
- **GPT** = OpenAI's models; **ChatGPT** = the app. Usable via web, desktop app, or code.
- Remember mini (fast/cheap) vs flagship (smartest); always verify anything important.
