# Lesson 02 — Your First Chat with ChatGPT

⏱️ **10 minutes** · Level: Beginner · Needs: a web browser

## 🧠 Theory (2 min)

The fastest way to use GPT is in your **web browser** at **chatgpt.com**. You type a message, ChatGPT replies, and the whole back-and-forth is a **conversation**. ChatGPT remembers everything *within that one conversation*, but a **new chat starts fresh**.

**Free vs. paid:** There's a free tier that's plenty for learning. Paid plans (ChatGPT Plus/Pro) give more usage and access to the most capable models. You can do this whole course on the free tier.

---

## 🛠️ Practice (6 min)

### Step 1 — Create your account

1. Open **Microsoft Edge** (or Chrome).
2. Go to **https://chatgpt.com**
3. Click **Sign up**. You can use a Google/Microsoft/Apple account or an email address.
4. Verify your email if asked, and complete the short sign-up.

### Step 2 — Send your first message

You'll see a text box at the bottom. Copy this in and press **Enter**:

```text
Hi ChatGPT! I'm brand new to AI. In 5 short bullet points, explain what you can help me with in everyday life. Keep it simple.
```

Read the reply. Congratulations — you just used AI. 🎉

### Step 3 — Have a real back-and-forth

In the **same chat**, send a follow-up:

```text
Great. Now pick bullet #1 and give me one concrete example I could try today.
```

Notice it built on its previous answer — that's conversation memory.

### Step 4 — Start a new chat

Click **New chat** (top-left). Ask:

```text
What did we just talk about?
```

It won't know — this is a fresh conversation. **Lesson:** keep related work in one chat; start a new chat for a new topic.

---

## 🗺️ Quick tour of the screen

| Area | What it does |
|------|--------------|
| **Left sidebar** | Recent chats, "New chat", GPTs and Projects |
| **Message box** | Where you type. **Shift+Enter** = new line, **Enter** = send |
| **Model picker** | Near the top; switch between fast and more capable models |
| **➕ / paperclip** | Add files and images (Lesson 4) |
| **🎤 mic** | Talk to ChatGPT instead of typing |

---

## ✅ Checkpoint

- [ ] You have a chatgpt.com account.
- [ ] You sent a message and got a reply.
- [ ] You understand a **new chat forgets** the old one.

---

## 🎯 Homework

Take your "most annoying text task" from Lesson 01 and just **describe it to ChatGPT**, ending with: *"Ask me any questions you need before helping."* Letting it ask first makes answers far better.

---

## 💡 Key takeaways

- chatgpt.com = zero-install way to use GPT.
- One conversation = shared memory; new chat = clean slate.
- **Shift+Enter** for multi-line prompts; you can also use the mic.
